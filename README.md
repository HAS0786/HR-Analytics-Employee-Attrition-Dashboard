# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Power BI. The objective is to identify employee groups with higher turnover rates and discover the factors associated with employee attrition.

The dashboard combines data cleaning, transformation, DAX calculations, and business-focused visualizations to support HR decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and impact organizational performance.

This project aims to answer:

* Which employee groups have the highest attrition rates?
* Which factors are associated with employee turnover?
* What actions can HR take to improve retention?

## Key Findings

* Employees working overtime exhibited significantly higher attrition rates.
* Low salary groups experienced the highest employee turnover.
* Poor work-life balance was associated with increased attrition.
* Young employees showed higher attrition than other age groups.
* The Sales department recorded the highest attrition rate.

---

## Recommendations

* Review overtime policies and workload distribution.
* Evaluate compensation strategies for low salary groups.
* Improve employee well-being initiatives.
* Strengthen employee retention programs for young employees.
* Monitor high-risk departments and employee segments.
